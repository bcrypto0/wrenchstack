About this sample

What this is
A written benchmark of how the 32 platforms on WrenchStack's pest control page price and package their software: entry prices and their basis, billing terms, price ladders, add-ons, quote terms, setup fees and contract terms. Built from WrenchStack's public data and the vendors' own pages. Delivered in writing only.
Data as of
29 October 2026. The prices in WrenchStack's data were read on the vendors' own pages between 29 September and 29 October 2026, mostly on pricing pages (exceptions in Section 6); plan details were read between 8 and 29 October 2026 and quote bases on 8 October 2026. WrenchStack's data records each of the 6 platforms shown as read on 29 October 2026 (Section 2).
Vendor section
In a purchased report, Section 5 sets the buying vendor's own published price, packaging and terms against the other platforms on the page. This sample names no vendor there: Section 5 describes what the section contains and shows one illustrative row built only from the market figures in Sections 1 and 2.
Independence
Whether a vendor bought a report is not an input to any listing, Fit Score, ranking, award or reputation flag, or to whether a product is listed. Every sale is listed in the report ledger at wrenchstack.com/methodology/#report-ledger, and reports are sold under the terms at wrenchstack.com/report-terms/. The independence rules are in Section 6.

Section 5

The vendor section

In a purchased report, this section covers the buying vendor's own product. This sample names no vendor here: it describes what the section contains and shows one illustrative row.

5.1 What the section contains

The buying vendor's own published price, packaging and terms, each set against the other platforms on the same trade page, using the figures in Sections 1 to 4. Pricing pages are compared with pricing pages, as in the rest of the report.

  • Published price. Its entry price with its basis and billing term, or that it publishes none, against the medians and ranges for the page and for its pricing model.
  • Price ladder and packaging. Its tiers, the users each includes, added-user prices, and what its pricing page puts in a higher tier or prices separately, against Section 3.
  • Quote basis. For a quote-only vendor, the basis its pages state, against the quote-only platforms in Section 4.
  • Setup and contract terms. Setup, onboarding or migration fees, contract term, cancellation and refunds as its pages state them, against Section 4. Where its pages do not state an item, the section says so.
  • Free trial. Its free trial, against the counts in Sections 1 and 2.

As the report terms (clause 2) state, the report uses vendors' public pages and what WrenchStack already publishes. Information another vendor sent WrenchStack privately is not included unless it already appears on that vendor's WrenchStack listing. Each published price is read from the vendor's own page while the report is written, where that page can be read. Where it cannot, the report says so and shows the page used and the date it was last read. Like the rest of the report, the section does not explain or recalculate any Fit Score and does not say how any vendor could change its position.

Section 6

Method, sources, limits and independence

How the figures were built, what was not checked, and the terms that keep reports separate from the site's rankings.

Sources

  • Platform data. WrenchStack's software directory as of 29 October 2026: 127 platforms, 32 of them listed for pest control. Pricing model, entry price, tiers, plan details, quote basis, free trial, QuickBooks listing, trade fit and best team size come from that data. Each platform's pricing note, which records what the vendor's page said on the date shown, is on its WrenchStack tool page.
  • Price dates. WrenchStack's verified dates for the 32 platforms are 29 October 2026 (10), 22 October (6), 8 October (9), 3 October (1) and 29 September (6). Plan details (each tier's price by billing term where the page states the term, and user rules) for 18 platforms were read on 8 October (6), 22 October (4) and 29 October 2026 (8), and quote bases for 7 on 8 October 2026; for 2 of the 18 WrenchStack's verified date is earlier than the plan-detail read. This excerpt uses the figures as WrenchStack's data records them, with the read dates shown. The page order is that of WrenchStack's pest control page as built from this data on 29 October 2026, and it matched the order computed from the data. WrenchStack's data records each of the 6 rows in Sections 2 to 4 as read on 29 October 2026, on its pricing page (KnowHow has none) and the other pages its pricing note cites.
  • Pages read. Most prices were read on the vendor's pricing page. Three platforms' prices were not read on a pricing page at all: ServSuite (FieldRoutes' ServSuite product page), KnowHow (no pricing page; its home, demo request, terms, Master Subscription Agreement and partner pages) and Vonigo (its pricing address resolves to the home page; its help page). One entry price rests on other pages of the vendor's site: MrTask's annual-billed prices, including its $33.25 entry, come from its restoration industry page and its home page FAQ (its monthly prices from its pricing page). Simpro's pricing page and GoCanvas's release notes (on its help centre) were read in a browser, because direct fetches of them return an error, and so were the yearly views of GorillaDesk's and QuoteIQ's pricing pages, which a direct fetch shows only in their monthly state (QuoteIQ's Terms of Service list the same yearly totals). Trial lengths for four platforms come from other pages of the vendor's site: Estimate Rocket's home page and Terms, and CompanyCam's restoration industry page (these two pricing pages give no length), GoCanvas's release notes and Mitti's preventive maintenance software guide (Section 2, notes b to d). Kickserv's pricing page states a 30-day trial and its restoration page 14 days.
  • Where WrenchStack's data and its notes differ. Trial counts take the length in WrenchStack's trial field only where the platform's pricing note records that length; the rule is the same for every platform. Contractor+ has no trial in the data, while its pricing note records the FAQ's 14-day trials of most premium features (read 22 October 2026); KnowHow has no trial in the data, while its note records that its Terms allow an occasional trial of a length it decides. Neither is counted, as the data lists no trial.

Calculations

  • Every count of platforms, median, price range and share of platforms (for example 81%) in this sample is WrenchStack's computation from its data as of 29 October 2026. Percentages in vendors' own words (for example a saving for annual billing) and the 20% weight of pricing transparency set by WrenchStack's methodology are not WrenchStack computations.
  • Medians use the entry prices above $0 in WrenchStack's data; quote-only platforms and free tiers are left out. The headline median over 18 values is a midpoint: $45.96, between GorillaDesk's $44.92 and Tradify's $47, shown as $46 as on the pest control page. The three medians by model are actual entries: per seat $39 (Praxedo and Synchroteam both enter at $39), per company $60 (Kickserv) and base plus users $59 (Housecall Pro). So are the segment medians in the Section 1 table, except the $53 for best team size starting at 1 or 2 people, a midpoint between Tradify's $47 and Housecall Pro's $59. The directory median, $63.38, is a midpoint, between entries of $63 and $63.75.
  • Pricing models follow WrenchStack's pricing model field: per seat (8), per company (3) and base plus users (7). Billing terms differ within each group.
  • Two of the 18 entry prices are WrenchStack's own arithmetic, not figures printed on the vendor's page: GorillaDesk's $44.92 is $539 a year divided by 12 (GorillaDesk's pricing page, read in a browser on 29 October, states $539 a year paid yearly for one route, and $49 a month billed monthly; added routes are priced separately), and Powered Now's $30.82 is £280 a year divided by 12 (£23.33), converted at 1.3207 USD per GBP (ECB reference rate via frankfurter.dev, 8 October 2026). Powered Now prices in GBP excluding VAT. Both are used as WrenchStack's data stores them, as on the pest control page. QuoteIQ's $25 is printed by QuoteIQ: its pricing page's yearly view, read in a browser on 29 October, shows Essentials at $25 a month, the monthly equivalent of $299.99 billed yearly, the yearly total its Terms of Service also list.
  • Percentages: 26 / 32 = 81.25%, shown as 81%.
  • Setup and contract terms (Section 4) count a pricing note only when it states the item for the subscription; wording that appears only in a free-trial offer is not counted, and neither is a note that lists such items as unanswered questions. A note that puts onboarding inside a quoted price counts as stating an onboarding charge (Smart Service's note: the written proposal includes software, onboarding and data conversion); a note that names onboarding without stating a fee either way is not counted (RazorSync lists it as a plan feature, Mitti as a contact-sales item), and neither is a term that covers free accounts only (Mitti's limit on no-charge services). CompanyCam is counted on its FAQ and Terms, not on the Cancel anytime. in its trial offer.
  • WrenchStack's pest control page splits a per-company plan across a 5-person team for its price bands. This report does not convert prices that way; it groups them by pricing model.

What this sample does not cover

  • No buyer interviews, win or loss data, quoted prices or WrenchStack traffic data.
  • No review-site ratings or other third-party scores, and no third-party price estimates.
  • No counts of features across platforms: WrenchStack's feature labels are not a controlled vocabulary, so counts would not compare like with like. Packaging is described from the pricing notes instead.
  • No vendor section: Section 5 describes it without naming a vendor.
  • Terms of service are not compared; setup and contract terms are as the pricing notes state them. Prices change; each figure is as read on the date it shows.

Independence

  1. Whether a vendor bought a report is not an input to any listing, Fit Score, ranking, award or reputation flag, or to whether a product is listed. No vendor has paid for placement, ranking or inclusion in the directory.
  2. Every report sale is listed in the report ledger at wrenchstack.com/methodology/#report-ledger, with the buyer's Fit Score, rank, award and reputation flag when it ordered and today. Refunded orders stay listed. A claim that a report sale affected a listing goes to hello@wrenchstack.com with the page URL; each one gets a written reply, and a confirmed problem is corrected on the page and noted in the report ledger.
  3. The report uses vendors' public pages and WrenchStack's published data. Prices re-read for a report go through the same second check of the vendor's page as routine re-checks and are updated on WrenchStack for every platform on that trade page, buyer or not, no later than the day the report is delivered. What WrenchStack finds on a vendor's own pages is corrected on that vendor's WrenchStack listing, with the date, whether or not it buys.
  4. Buying a report gives no preview of what WrenchStack publishes, no advance notice of changes and no say over what WrenchStack publishes.
  5. WrenchStack does not quote a price, send an invoice or take an order while an evaluation, correction, pricing question, dispute or reputation-flag review is open with that vendor, or within 30 days after the latest listing verdict, correction or reputation-flag email it sent that vendor. As its methodology and for-vendors pages state, A question we ask a vendor counts as closed once it has gone 30 days without an answer; the listing stays as the vendor's public pages describe it, and a later answer is handled as a correction.
  6. A report is not advice on how to rank on WrenchStack. It does not explain or recalculate any Fit Score and does not say how any vendor could change its position. Whether a product publishes a price is one of the three Fit Score factors (pricing transparency, 20%, set out at wrenchstack.com/methodology/); the report describes published pricing for every platform and computes no score.
  7. Reports are sold under the terms at wrenchstack.com/report-terms/.

Corrections to any figure are welcome in writing, with a source.

WrenchStack is an independent directory comparing software for US trade contractors. Sample report, October 2026; data as of 29 October 2026.
